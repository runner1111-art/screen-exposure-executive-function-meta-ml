# Analysis data

- `Meta-analysis_data_full_age_updated.xlsx` is the locked effect-level workbook used by the R and Python analyses. Its `Meta_Data` sheet contains 376 rows from 69 quantitatively represented reports, including identifiers, harmonized effect sizes, sampling variances, moderators, provenance, conversion method, source location, verification status and adjudicated analysis tier. The `Extraction_Adjudication` sheet preserves unresolved or sensitivity-only candidates, and `Fulltext_Status` records document availability.
- `Table_1_final_verified.xlsx` contains 103 included reports and is used to derive the geographic distribution. All 103 reports have a design-specific quality assessment; two unavailable-full-text records were excluded at eligibility and are not listed in Table 1.
- `ML_proxy_sensitivity_dataset.csv` is the frozen input for the separately labelled proxy-statistic sensitivity analysis. It is an analysis input, not a model-run intermediate.

Missing values retained as `NR` indicate that the item could not be obtained from the article or available supplementary material. `Expanded_Primary_Model = 1` identifies the 376 effects from 69 reports eligible for the expanded convertible-effect analysis. `Conversion_Class` records whether an effect was reported directly or derived from an odds ratio, standardized mean difference, group means, test statistic, explained variance or a verified standardized coefficient. The three core-domain models use 261 expanded effects from 43 reports and 45 cohort/sample clusters. `Primary_Model = 1` preserves the original strict set of 153 effects overall and 104 core-domain effects from 19 reports for sensitivity analysis. `Expanded_Analysis_Audit` and `Fulltext_Status` document the two formal full-text exclusions and the removal of their ten secondary-source rows before analysis.
