# Analysis data

- `Meta-analysis_data_full_age_updated.xlsx` is the locked effect-level workbook used by the R analysis. Its `Meta_Data` sheet contains identifiers, harmonized effect sizes, sampling variances and moderators.
- `Table_1_final_verified.xlsx` is the final full-text-verified study-characteristics and quality-assessment table. It is also used to derive the geographic distribution of included reports.

Missing values retained as `NR` indicate that the item could not be obtained from the article or available supplementary material. The analysis scripts apply their prespecified eligibility fields and do not assume that every extracted row belongs to every analysis set.
